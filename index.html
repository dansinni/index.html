01What I know about Cognition

An agent lab built for the enterprise

The company, the product family, and why large travel and hospitality companies buy it.

The company

Cognition describes itself as an applied AI lab building end-to-end software agents. It raised over $2B at a $48B valuation in September 20261, with run-rate revenue up from $492M in May to almost $900M2. Devin writes most of Cognition's own code3.

The difference

Cognition invests as much in forward-deployed engineering and AI enablement as in core R&D, accelerates the whole software lifecycle, and absorbs deployment complexity: VPC deployment, permissions for 100,000+ developers, COBOL as well as Python. It is also model-independent, which large buyers value for supplier continuity19.

The motion

Usage-based pricing on Agent Compute Units aligns cost with work done, so accounts grow as teams roll Devin out. Cognition guarantees Devin delivers at least as much engineering value as a customer consumes8. Customers include Citi, NVIDIA and GE Aerospace2.

02Why Travel & Hospitality

Three pressures every executive in this industry feels

None of them is a coding problem. They are capacity, risk and speed problems, and Devin is strongest exactly where this industry is weakest: old code, security debt and incident response.

Downtime is a P&L event

Boards remember the numbers. An IT failure at an airline or resort shows up in the quarter, the stock and a DOT or regulator inquiry.

07Deal

Where these deals stall, and how we qualify them

Six stalls we plan for from the first meeting, and the MEDDPICC checklist every opportunity runs against.

"Our code is too old and too custom"

We start with DeepWiki on one TPF or COBOL module in week one, with no code changes, and show the Mercedes-Benz result9.

"Security won't let an agent near this"

Single-tenant on AWS, each session in its own VM, every change as a reviewed PR10. The CISO joins at M2, not at signature.

"Not during peak season"

We time the land for shoulder season and start with work that never touches a peak release: documentation, tests and security fixes.

The pilot drifts

No open-ended pilots. One use case, written success criteria and the expansion terms agreed before work starts.

Usage pricing worries finance

We forecast ACU consumption from the first six weeks and lead with Cognition's value guarantee8.

Only engineers are involved

The CIO or COO is in the room by M2, and the business case prices outage hours, consultant spend and open reqs.

MEDDPICC checklist

Updated after every call and reviewed with each AE weekly. Select a letter to flip it to the proof required before a deal counts in the forecast.

Commit ruleA deal enters commit only when the economic buyer has confirmed the metrics, the use case has met written success criteria, the paper process is dated in a mutual plan, and the champion has been tested.

Why travel and hospitality from FSI

FSI is the hardest buyer in enterprise software: the heaviest security review, the most legacy debt and the longest cycles. Travel and hospitality shares all three, with TPF instead of COBOL cores and PCI and FTC orders instead of bank regulators. What transfers is the motion: selling to engineering leaders, multithreading large organizations, getting through risk review and turning modernization into a business case.

Why Cognition

Most AI coding tools sell seats to developers. Cognition sells finished engineering work to the enterprise and backs it with forward-deployed engineers, which is the motion I have run for a decade in cloud-native and AI-assisted modernization. Devin's usage grows with outcomes, so a good land in this industry becomes a large account, and this industry has more legacy code and peak-season risk than almost any other.
